Merchandise rollout to 2,000+ stores

The Coca-Cola Company, Hypercare Accelerator, 2025–2026

ResultAll 2,000+ stores went live. The delay was recovered in batches: the quickest accounts in about 2 weeks, the slowest in 3.
Situation
A rollout of 160,000+ units slipped 2–3 weeks across minimarket accounts. Stores could not receive the merchandise until each account registered the product barcodes (PLU), and production volume was very high.
My role
Coordinated allocation, sampling, vendor production monitoring and delivery to stores, and kept quotations and budget aligned.
What I did
Ran daily calls with stakeholders, escalated blockers to the director and sent progress reports by email.
What I learned
It was my first rollout of this size, so I could not predict every blocker. Now I coordinate earlier and more firmly, with written updates and early escalation.

How I would run a packaging project

This is my approach, not past work. I haven’t managed a packaging project yet. It applies what I did on the rollout, with vendors and with quotations. I’m also learning Lark Base to keep trackers in the team’s own system.

Brief

Confirm needs, pricing and timeline with the client in writing, then turn them into clear factory instructions.

Quote

Prepare the quotation, track cost against budget and keep documents current.

Sample

Send briefs, collect feedback and log every revision in one tracker.

Produce

Monitor production against the timeline and escalate early when something slips.

Deliver

Coordinate with logistics, keep the client updated and close every action item.
